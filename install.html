📋 Installation & First-Time Setup Steps

  1. Plug your ESP32 into your computer's USB port and click ⚡ Install Curtain Studio Firmware above.
  2. Select your ESP32's serial port from the popup list and click Install. (If it stays on "Connecting...", hold down the BOOT button on the ESP32 while starting the install).
  3. Once installation completes, connect your phone or PC to the Wi-Fi Access Point:
    SSID: Curtain-Lights • Password: 12345678
  4. Open your browser to http://192.168.4.1
  5. Click ⚙️ CONFIG → 📶 Wi-Fi, scan for your home network, enter your password, and click Save & Connect.

🔌 Default Pin Assignment (Configurable in Web UI)

  • LED Curtain MOSFET Gate Output: GPIO 4 (via 200Ω resistor to MOSFET Gate)
  • Infrared (IR) Receiver Input: GPIO 15 or GPIO 16 (Configurable in Config → Pins & HW)
  • External Power Relay Pin: Configurable (Disabled / Active LOW / Active HIGH)
